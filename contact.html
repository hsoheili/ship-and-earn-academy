Book a free 15-minute fit call

A fit call is a short, honest conversation. No slides, no pressure. To book one, email hello@shipandearn.academy with the subject line "Fit call" and two or three times that suit you. We hold calls Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00–20:00 Pacific.

What we will ask you

  • What have you built so far, even if it is one page from a tutorial?
  • How many hours a week can you honestly protect? We plan around 8, not 40.
  • What does "earning" mean to you — 200 dollars once, or 600 a month by summer?
  • Do you have school or exam dates that will eat three weeks of the cohort?

What you get out of it

  • A straight answer on whether the next cohort is right for you, or whether you should do six more weeks of free practice first.
  • A one-page week-by-week plan for your own hours, emailed after the call.
  • A price, including any student discount, with nothing hidden behind a deposit.
  • Two specific things to build before the cohort starts, if you enrol.

We say "not yet" to roughly one in five callers. Usually that means you need a working laptop, or you have two hours a week and the cohort needs eight. We will tell you what to change and invite you to the following intake.

What the 12 weeks look like

  • Weeks 1–4: build

    HTML, CSS, layout, forms, and accessibility basics. You ship four small sites, one a week, and get written review on each.

  • Weeks 5–8: make it real

    JavaScript for the things clients actually ask for. You rebuild one of your week 1–4 projects for a real person — a club, a relative, a local shop.

  • Weeks 9–12: earn

    Pricing, quotes, scope, invoices, and a portfolio page that answers a buyer's questions. Most students send their first paid quote in week 10 or 11.

The refund window

You can ask for a full refund up to the end of week 2, for any reason, by one email. No exit interview. After week 2 we cannot refund, because your seat and your reviewer's hours are committed. If life genuinely derails you mid-cohort, we will move you to the next intake once, free.

Frequently asked questions

How old do I need to be?

16 or older to enrol on your own. From 14 to 15 you are welcome with a parent or guardian on the enrolment email, and we ask them to sit in on the fit call. There is no upper limit — our oldest student so far was 41, though the material is written for the 16–26 range and the examples reflect that.

What equipment do I need?

A laptop or desktop you can install a code editor on, running Windows, macOS or Linux, with at least 4 GB of RAM. A Chromebook in developer mode works but is harder; tell us and we will point you at the setup guide. A tablet or phone alone is not enough — you need a real keyboard and a real browser with developer tools. Internet fast enough for video calls helps, but every live session is recorded.

Do I need a computer science background?

No. Roughly 60 per cent of our students have never taken a CS class. What you do need is comfort with being confused for an hour before something clicks, and enough maths to handle percentages when you price a job. If you have a CS degree already, the first four weeks will feel slow — ask about the accelerated start.

What about time zones?

Live sessions run twice each week: 18:30 Pacific for the Americas and 08:00 Pacific for Europe, Africa and West Asia. You attend whichever suits you, and you can switch week to week. Both are recorded and posted within four hours. Code review is written, not live, so it never depends on your time zone. Students from 14 countries have finished a cohort this way.

Will I definitely earn money?

No, and anyone who promises that is selling you something. About two thirds of students who finish all 12 weeks earn their first money within three months of the end. The students who do not earn almost always stopped sending quotes. The course gives you the skills and the exact scripts; the sending is yours.

Can I pause mid-cohort?

Once, yes. Email us and we will hold your place for the next intake at no extra cost. Your project files and reviews stay available the whole time.
